Privacy Policy Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS Program Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled for the Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS program. Information collected The program may store a recipient's mobile phone number, opt-in and opt-out status, messaging preferences, and limited operational information needed to deliver household alerts and reminders. How information is used Information is used only to operate the household notification program, deliver requested messages, maintain consent and opt-out records, and troubleshoot message delivery. SMS privacy and sharing Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are used only for this messaging program and are not transferred to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate and deliver the messaging service, such as telecommunications carriers and messaging infrastructure providers, solely to provide the service. Message frequency and charges Message frequency varies based on configured reminders, household events, weather events, and infrastructure conditions. Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and service plan. Retention Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the program, maintain consent records, or comply with applicable legal or carrier requirements. Opting out Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Contact Questions about this SMS program may be sent by replying HELP to +1 817 518 6732.
