Privacy Policy Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS Program Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions This privacy policy explains how information is handled for the Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS program. Information collected The program may store a recipient's mobile phone number, opt-in status, messaging preferences, and limited operational information needed to deliver household alerts and reminders. How information is used Information is used only to operate the household notification program, deliver requested messages, maintain opt-in and opt-out status, and troubleshoot message delivery. Sharing of information Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to deliver the messaging service, such as telecommunications and messaging infrastructure providers, solely for the purpose of operating the program. Retention Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the program, maintain consent records, or comply with applicable legal or carrier requirements. Opting out Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Contact Questions about this SMS program may be sent by replying HELP to +1 817 518 6732.
