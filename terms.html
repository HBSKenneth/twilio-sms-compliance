SMS Terms & Conditions Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS Program Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Program description Kenneth Havins Household Alerts is a private, non-marketing SMS program for family and household members who explicitly opt in. Messages may include household reminders, task and list notifications, weather alerts, home infrastructure alerts, network or server notifications, power or UPS alerts, and similar household operational notifications. Consent and enrollment Recipients are enrolled only after giving affirmative consent through the documented verbal opt-in process. By opting in, the recipient agrees to receive recurring automated SMS messages from Kenneth Havins Household Alerts. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Message frequency Message frequency varies depending on configured reminders, household events, weather events, and infrastructure conditions. Message and data rates Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and service plan. Opt-out Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. After an opt-out request, the recipient may receive a final confirmation message and will not receive additional program messages unless the recipient later provides new consent. Help Reply HELP for help. Support is available through the messaging program at +1 817 518 6732. Privacy Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
