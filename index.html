Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS Program Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Kenneth Havins Household Alerts is a private, non-marketing SMS notification program for family and household members who explicitly choose to receive messages. Types of messages Messages may include household reminders, task and list notifications, weather alerts, home infrastructure alerts, network or server notifications, power or UPS alerts, and other household operational notifications. How to opt in Enrollment is by direct verbal consent. A mobile number is not added until the recipient affirmatively agrees to receive SMS messages after receiving the disclosure below. Verbal opt-in disclosure: “By agreeing, you consent to receive recurring automated SMS messages from Kenneth Havins Household Alerts about household reminders, weather alerts, task and list notifications, and home infrastructure or service alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Please review the Privacy Policy at https://hbskenneth.github.io/twilio-sms-compliance/privacy.html and the Terms & Conditions at https://hbskenneth.github.io/twilio-sms-compliance/terms.html. Do you agree to receive these SMS messages?” The recipient must give an affirmative response before the mobile number is enrolled. Message frequency Message frequency varies based on configured reminders, household events, weather events, and infrastructure conditions. Help and opt-out Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages. Support is available through the messaging program at +1 817 518 6732. Message and data rates may apply.
