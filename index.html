Kenneth Havins Household Alerts SMS Program Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Kenneth Havins Household Alerts is a private, non-marketing SMS notification program for family and household members who explicitly choose to receive messages. Types of messages Messages may include household reminders, task and list notifications, weather alerts, home infrastructure alerts, network or server notifications, power or UPS alerts, and other household operational notifications. How to opt in Enrollment is by direct verbal consent. Before a phone number is added to the program, the recipient is told that they are agreeing to receive recurring automated household alerts and reminders from Kenneth Havins, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. A recipient's number is added only after the recipient gives affirmative consent. Message frequency Message frequency varies based on configured reminders, household events, weather events, and infrastructure conditions. Help and opt-out Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages. Support is available through the messaging program at +1 817 518 6732. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
